ROASTERY & METHOD

Coffee Craftsmanship

From high-altitude micro-lots to controlled temperature extraction.

01 / Sourcing

Single-Origin Transparency

We work directly with smallholder farmers in Yirgacheffe, Huehuetenango, and Tarrazú, ensuring fair pricing and sustainable agricultural practices.

02 / Roasting

Light to Medium Profiles

Our small-batch roasts preserve floral aromatics, bright acidity, and sweet fruit notes without heavy bitterness.

03 / Extraction

Precision Water Chemistry

Every morning we calibrate espresso parameters, water mineral content, and grind particle distribution for consistent balance.

PHILOSOPHY

Simplicity, clarity, and genuine hospitality.

Atila Coffee was established as an antidote to fast-paced routines. We believe a cup of specialty coffee should be an intentional experience—from the fragrance of fresh grounds to the quiet environment in which it is served.

Our spaces are built with natural timber, brushed warm steel, and subtle ambient lighting, offering a peaceful atmosphere for focus, reflection, or conversation.
